## [MS Paint and Photos Silently Embed Invisible GUID Watermarks in Local AI Output](https://xusheng.dev/posts/reversing/mspaint_invisible_watermark/main/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Reverse engineering revealed that Microsoft Paint and Photos silently embed an invisible watermark containing a unique identifier \(GUID\) into images processed with local AI features. This watermarking happens automatically in the background without user notice or consent, even when using purely local AI processing. This discovery raises severe privacy and security concerns because embedding unique tracking identifiers into generated or edited media undermines online anonymity and enables tracing files directly back to individual user accounts. It highlights growing friction around tech companies quietly injecting persistent metadata under the banner of AI provenance. While users can toggle off visible AI watermarks in app settings, the hidden steganographic watermark cannot be disabled. Technical details are still being examined regarding how resilient this embedded GUID is against common image modifications, such as JPEG re-compression or cropping.

hackernews · ComputerGuru · Aug 24, 15:28 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421158)

**Background**: Digital watermarking and steganography are techniques used to hide data, such as metadata or unique signatures, within media files without visibly altering them. Microsoft has recently introduced local AI-powered capabilities—like image generation and background removal—into standard Windows desktop applications like Paint and Photos.

**Discussion**: The community expressed strong concern over privacy and user tracking, emphasizing that unique GUIDs could allow copyright subpoenas or legal actions to easily unmask anonymous users via their Microsoft accounts. Commenters also questioned the technical durability of the invisible steganography against lossy compression and criticized Microsoft for sloppy implementation and mandatory metadata injection.
