## [New Mexico Court Orders Meta to Pay $567 Million over Youth Mental Health Harms](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/06/new-mexico-court-meta) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A New Mexico court has ordered Meta to pay $567 million under state public nuisance laws for mental health harms inflicted on underage users. The court decision also requires Meta to implement operational changes to better protect minors using its social media platforms. This ruling establishes a significant legal precedent by holding social media companies accountable for platform design choice under public nuisance laws, similar to historic cases against the tobacco and opioid industries. If upheld, it could trigger a wave of similar state-level lawsuits across the United States. The lawsuit was brought under New Mexico public nuisance law \(NMSA 1978 § 30-8-1\), asserting Meta knowingly maintained features injurious to public health and welfare. Given New Mexico&\#x27;s population of just over 2 million people, the financial penalty represents a exceptionally huge localized judgment relative to Meta&\#x27;s state-level revenue.

hackernews · boplicity · Aug 7, 00:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49204352)

**Background**: Public nuisance laws have historically been used to address physical environmental harms or public hazards, but prosecutors are now using them to challenge addictive digital products like social media and e-cigarettes. By framing algorithmic recommendations and addictive product design as public nuisances, litigants aim to bypass legal shields like Section 230 that typically protect tech companies from liability for user-generated content.
