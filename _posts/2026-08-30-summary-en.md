## [Tencent Open-Sources Hy4 Preview Flagship LLM Featuring Recursive Self-Improvement](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent has released and open-sourced Tencent Hy4 preview, a flagship Mixture-of-Experts \(MoE\) large language model featuring 770 billion total parameters and 49 billion active parameters per token. Notably, the model actively participated in optimizing its own training pipelines, data strategies, and low-level operators, establishing an early-stage recursive self-improvement loop. Hy4 preview demonstrates a practical milestone for automated training optimization and recursive self-improvement in open-weights models. Its immediate popularity on platforms like OpenRouter highlights strong developer interest in highly efficient, open-weights frontier models. The model features a context window exceeding 1 million tokens and utilizes a Mixture-of-Experts architecture to optimize inference efficiency. On third-party API platforms like OpenRouter, it offers aggressive pricing with low prompt caching fees, fueling rapid adoption.

## [Dan Luu on &\#x27;Bug Blindness&\#x27; in Software Engineering and User Experience](https://danluu.com/bug-blind/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Software engineer Dan Luu published an essay examining &\#x27;bug blindness,&\#x27; explaining how both developers and users become so accustomed to software flaws and poor user experiences that they cease to perceive them as problems. Understanding bug blindness is critical for engineering teams striving for product quality, as team familiarity can blind creators to usability defects that degrade customer satisfaction. It underlines the fundamental disconnect between how builders interact with systems versus how end users experience them. The analysis highlights how enterprise tools like Blackboard, Epic, and SharePoint frequently exhibit poor quality because software purchasers are distinct from end users, weakening market incentives for intuitive design.

## [NASA Prepares the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope for Launch](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/roman-space-telescope/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

NASA is finalizing preparation for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, an infrared observatory designed for large-scale cosmic survey missions. The telescope features an immediate open data policy, releasing all processed scientific observations to the public without any embargo period. With a field of view 100 times larger than the Hubble Space Telescope while maintaining comparable image sharpness, Roman will rapidly map billions of celestial bodies to study dark energy and exoplanets. Its zero-embargo data model will democratize astronomical research, allowing researchers and citizen scientists worldwide to analyze up to 1.4 TB of raw data per day immediately. Built using a donated 2.4-meter primary mirror optics assembly, the space telescope carries a 300.8-megapixel Wide-Field Instrument \(WFI\) and a high-contrast Coronagraph Instrument \(CGI\). It is scheduled to launch toward the Sun–Earth L2 orbit aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket.

hackernews · JumpCrisscross · Aug 29, 15:48 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49490870)

**Background**: While telescopes like Hubble and JWST are designed to zoom in on narrow target regions in high resolution, wide-field survey telescopes scan vast areas of the sky quickly to compile massive datasets. Named after NASA&\#x27;s first chief astronomer Nancy Grace Roman, the mission was rated as the highest priority for space astronomy in the 2010 National Research Council Decadal Survey.
