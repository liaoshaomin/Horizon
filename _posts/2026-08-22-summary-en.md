Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory have released the largest 2D map of the universe to date, featuring an interactive online sky viewer. The comprehensive dataset visualizes billions of galaxies across vast expanses of the night sky. This map provides astronomers and the public with an unprecedentedly detailed resource for studying cosmic structure and galaxy distribution. It represents a monumental achievement in astronomical data visualization that could remain a primary cosmological reference for years. Accessible via the Legacy Survey Sky Viewer, the platform allows users to pan and zoom through astronomical imagery seamlessly. The map reveals that areas previously perceived as dark void are actually populated with distant galaxies upon closer inspection.

Projects led by research institutes like Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory combine vast amounts of optical image data to build comprehensive reference maps for understanding the structure and history of the universe.
